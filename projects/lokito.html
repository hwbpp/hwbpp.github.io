Lokito

A public-facility finder for Jakarta, built on a real OpenStreetMap snapshot. It starts with the nearest mapped toilet, shows what is known about it, and hands off to map directions.

Status
In development
Type
Venture Creation course
Team
5 people · Hugo as CTO
Stack
Python, Streamlit, pandas, SQLite

01Problem

People can find a mall or station on a map but still have to work out whether it has the facility they need. Lokito starts from the need, a nearby toilet, with prayer spaces and drinking water as secondary options.

02Data / Context

A snapshot of the Jakarta administrative area downloaded from OpenStreetMap on 14 September 2026.

  • 5,824mapped objects: 141 toilets, 5,645 places of worship, 38 drinking-water points
  • 92.6%of records have a name
  • < 1%have access, opening-hours, wheelchair, or fee details

The data-quality audit showed that locations are well covered but practical details are mostly missing, so the app shows unknowns openly instead of guessing.

03Approach

  1. Overpass APIOpenStreetMap query
  2. Raw snapshotKept unchanged
  3. Normalize and auditpandas, completeness
  4. Streamlit appMap, list, details

Raw data stays separate from the normalized records the app uses.

  • Nearest first: facilities ranked by straight-line distance from a starting point, with filters for radius, wheelchair access, fee, access, and religion.
  • Venue context: 1,170 facilities matched to nearby malls, stations, and fuel stations by location.
  • Trust view: a "Data & trust" page shows sources, coverage gaps, and timestamps; visitor observations are held as pending review.
